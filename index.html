Frequently Asked Questions

What makes NeoHR different from other career coaching services?

NeoHR combines 20+ years of corporate HR and recruiting experience with the DrivePath Methodology™, a proprietary 4-stage framework. Christina is a DDI Certified Behavioral-Based Interview Facilitator, a 2026 Worldwide Women's Association Professional Excellence honoree, and Top Rated on Upwork.

How do I know which program is right for me?

Take the Career Archetype Quiz on this page, or book a free 30-minute strategy session with Christina. She'll help you identify exactly where you are and which path will get you to your goal fastest.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. At checkout you can choose to pay over time with Klarna or Afterpay, subject to their approval. You will see the payment schedule before you confirm, and your program starts right away. Afterpay covers purchases up to $4,000, and Klarna is available for every program, including Executive C-Suite. Questions? Ask during your free strategy session or email info@neohrus.com.

What is your refund policy?

Coaching programs are fully refundable within 7 days of purchase if you haven't had your first session. Quick-win services are fully refundable until your session is held or work begins. Full details for every service are in our Refund Policy.

How quickly can I expect to see results?

Many clients see early signs of progress, like more interview callbacks and recruiter views on LinkedIn, within the first few weeks of their program. Every search is different, so timelines vary with your field, level, and market.

Do you work with clients outside the U.S.?

Yes. NeoHR operates fully remote, so we deliver 1:1 coaching, career playbooks, and corporate solutions virtually to clients and partner organizations anywhere in the world.

What industries do you specialize in?

NeoHR specializes in life sciences, biologics manufacturing, behavioral health and healthcare, clinical research, and agriculture, with additional experience in sustainable energy and digital production. Christina's cross-industry HR expertise allows her to serve professionals across virtually any sector.

Is the free strategy session really free?

Yes, completely. The free 30-minute strategy session is a no-pressure call where Christina listens to your situation and helps you map out the right path. No pitch, just value.

What is the DrivePath Methodology™?

DrivePath is NeoHR's proprietary 4-stage career transformation framework: Drive (your purpose, strengths, and career vision), Path (your strategic roadmap and positioning), Ascent (your tools: resume, LinkedIn, and personal brand), and Impact (land your role, negotiate your worth, and grow). Every program is built around this framework.

How is Fractional Recruiting priced?

Fractional Recruiting is priced as a weekly rate based on the hours and timeline your hiring needs require, from a 10-hour/week Rapid Hire package for urgent single roles up to a fully embedded 40-hour/week recruiter. Book a free 30-minute Recruiting Fit Call and we'll match you to the right tier.
